Windows x64 or ARM64.

Corresponding source delivery (same Release as these binaries):
https://github.com/rumoii/streamfirefly/releases/tag/v1.0.2
Download FFMPEG-SOURCE-MANIFEST.json and every
StreamFirefly-ffmpeg-source-1.0.2.tar.gz.partNNN attachment.
Concatenate parts in numeric order, verify the archive SHA-256 from the
manifest, and extract the archive. Its README.md contains build instructions,
source provenance, licenses and the build-tool reproducibility boundary.

The installer build records the exact binary archive name and SHA-256 in
tools/download-ffmpeg.ps1. FFMPEG-LICENSE.txt is copied from that verified
archive. FFmpeg is an independent executable invoked by StreamFirefly and is
distributed under the GNU General Public License.
